About Jym

  • Systems Engineering: Cross-platform systems development across Windows, Linux, and macOS. Long-term practitioner in .NET/C# system architectures, endpoint telemetry, and graph-database optimization.
  • Agentic AI Systems: Designed, implemented, and benchmarked multi-agent fleets capable of autonomous end-to-end workflows beyond code generation, spanning automated video production, binary reverse engineering, threat analysis, detection, deception, and active containment.
